About

Riley Hoff is an artist based in Brookyln, NY. They have a BA in Studio Arts from University of Vermont. Their work explores themes of memory, the human experience and identity, primarily though the medium of oil painting and charcoal. Additional mixed media and digital works can be found in the 'misc' section.
